Why players like it

No accounts, no tracking, no clutter. A scoreboard that keeps up with the game, and now one that everyone in the room can share.

Fast scoring

Tap a number for a single, hold for a double or triple, fix any dart with a tap. No menus between throws.

Play together

One game on everyone's own iPhone or iPad, in the same room or over several days. Friends find your game nearby, scan a code, or open an invitation.

On the big screen

Host a game on Apple TV and everyone joins with a four-digit code, or AirPlay the board from your phone. Readable from across the room.

On your wrist

Score from your Apple Watch, with your iPhone or without it. Start games, add players, and join a game on a TV with its code.

Everything a scoreboard should do

1 to 5 players

Player profiles with photos, turn order, and per-player marks. Practice alone or score the whole crew.

Every cricket variant

Standard, points, cut-throat, Wild Card Cricket with random numbers, and Tactics with doubles, triples and three in a bed. Marks drawn the way they are on a chalkboard.

301, 501 and more

Double in, double out, and scoring by turn total for people who add up in their heads.

Computer opponent

Four skill levels from beginner to pro, for practice when nobody's around.

Remote play

One game on everyone's own device, through iCloud. Join from the nearby list, a QR code or an invitation.

Apple TV and big screen

Host a game on Apple TV with a four-digit code, or AirPlay the board from your phone.

Stats and history

Averages, marks per round, checkouts and 180s across games, and every turn of every game, kept in your own iCloud.

Free, with Pro

The scoreboard is free, with one small card on Home suggesting another indie app. Pro removes it and adds remote play, the big screen, the computer opponent, stats and the Live Activity for $0.99 a month or $9.99 a year. No accounts, no tracking.

Dart Scoreboard on Apple TV

The Apple TV app turns the TV by the board into the scoreboard. It hosts the game, and everyone scores from their own iPhone, iPad or Apple Watch.

Host with a code

Pick the game and its rules on the TV and open the room. It shows a four-digit code and a QR code, and friends are in within seconds.

A board for the whole room

Scores, marks and whose throw it is, with recent turns and stats beside the board, all sized to read from the throw line.

It calls the game

Sound effects and an announcer that calls each turn and the scores, in the voice you choose.

Your rules

Cricket, Tactics, Wild Card, 301 up to 1001 and Shanghai, with your own target numbers. Let anyone score, or only each player's own phone.

The board on the big screen

The scoreboard, the waiting room with its code, and the Home screen, on Apple TV.

Dart Scoreboard on Apple Watch

Leave the phone in your pocket, or at home. The watch keeps score on its own, from the throw line.

Score from your wrist

Tap a number for a single, hold for a double or triple, and tap a dart to fix it. Undo, redo and next player are one swipe away.

The whole board

Swipe over for the scoreboard: marks, scores and every turn, just as on the phone. A tap on the wrist tells you when someone has won.

On its own

Start Cricket, Tactics, Wild Card, Shanghai, 301 or 501 without your iPhone. Choose your numbers, add a player by typing a name, or pick an unfinished game back up.

In step with your devices

Games you finish or save on the watch reach History on your iPhone and iPad through iCloud. Near an Apple TV, type its four-digit code and score your darts from your wrist.

The game on your wrist

The scoring keys, the scoreboard and the turn-total keypad, on Apple Watch.

About the developer

Jon Hill

Developer

My name is Jon and I like to play darts. I built Dart Scoreboard Pro because I was frustrated with the buggy, overly complex dart apps in the App Store — I wanted something anyone could open and start playing immediately. More than a decade later, that’s still the whole idea.

Why players like it

No ads, no accounts, no clutter. A scoreboard that keeps up with the game, and now one that everyone in the room can share.

Fast scoring

Tap a number for a single, hold for a double or triple, fix any dart with a tap. No menus between throws.

Play together

One game on everyone's own iPhone or iPad, in the same room or over several days. Friends find your game nearby, scan a code, or open an invitation.

On the big screen

Host a game on Apple TV and everyone joins with a four-digit code, or AirPlay the board from your phone. Readable from across the room.

On your wrist

Score from your Apple Watch during any game on your phone, start games with your players, and join a game on a TV with its code.

Everything a scoreboard should do

1 to 5 players

Player profiles with photos, turn order, and per-player marks. Practice alone or score the whole crew.

Every cricket variant

Standard, points, cut-throat, Wild Card Cricket with random numbers, and Tactics with doubles, triples and three in a bed. Marks drawn the way they are on a chalkboard.

301, 501 and more

Double in, double out, and scoring by turn total for people who add up in their heads.

Computer opponent

Four skill levels from beginner to pro, for practice when nobody's around.

Remote play

One game on everyone's own device, through iCloud. Join from the nearby list, a QR code or an invitation.

Apple TV and big screen

Host a game on Apple TV with a four-digit code, or AirPlay the board from your phone.

Stats and history

Averages, marks per round, checkouts and 180s across games, and every turn of every game, kept in your own iCloud.

Free, with Pro

The scoreboard is free. Pro adds remote play, the big screen, the computer opponent, stats and the Live Activity for $0.99 a month or $9.99 a year. No ads, no accounts.

About the developer

Jon Hill

Developer

My name is Jon and I like to play darts. I built Dart Scoreboard Pro because I was frustrated with the buggy, overly complex dart apps in the App Store — I wanted something anyone could open and start playing immediately. More than a decade later, that’s still the whole idea.

Frequently asked questions

Cricket (standard, points and cut-throat), Wild Card Cricket with random numbers, Tactics with doubles, triples and three in a bed, 301 and 501 with optional double in and double out, and Shanghai. Cricket and Shanghai also let you pick your own numbers.

One person starts a game and friends join from their own iPhone or iPad: from the nearby list on the same Wi‑Fi, by scanning a QR code, or with an invitation. Everyone scores their own players, and the game keeps itself in sync through iCloud, in the same room or over several days.

It hosts a game on the big screen. The TV shows a four-digit code; everyone joins from a phone or watch and scores from their own device, and the TV shows the board, recent turns and stats. You can also AirPlay the board from your phone.

Score from your wrist during any game on your phone, start a game with your players, pick an unfinished game back up, or join a game on a TV with its code.

Tap the dart to change or remove it, or use Undo. Redo puts it back.
